Research date: 2026-11-16. Method note: about 75 search/fetch calls; the session's web-search budget ran out before every lead could be chased. Several hosts could not be fetched from this machine (kucoin.com, htx.com, everstake.one returned a certificate mismatch; dorahacks.io returned HTTP 405; dexscreener.com 403; x.com 402; hackathon.monad.xyz is login-walled). Wherever a fact rests only on a search-result snippet rather than a fetched page, it is marked "(snippet only)". Nothing below is invented; anything not found is written "not confirmed".

## Which hackathons did these ecosystems run in 2026 with winners announced?

### Takeaway

### Inferences
- Monad is by far the most active of these ecosystems in 2026 (at least three winner-announced hackathons plus Blitz city events); the other named chains appear to have run few or no flagship hackathons with public winner lists this year.

- Sei, Berachain, Abstract, Sonic (2026), Plasma, Ink, Story, Initia, MegaETH: searches ("<chain> hackathon 2026 winners", combined variants) returned no 2026 winners. Absence of evidence only; DoraHacks/Devfolio/HackQuest listings were not directly browsable and the search budget ran out.

## Profiles of each gaming or game-like winner

### Takeaway
Across about 25 game-like winners, almost none on the named high-performance chains implements cryptographic hidden information; they rely on public state, real-time price feeds, dual-signed results or a server. The only winners with real encrypted state are in the adjacent Inco game jam (encrypted Uno hands, fog-of-war extraction, sealed murder-mystery picks, hidden bridge layout), which shows exactly the design space an FHEVM entry can occupy on its own.

**Moltiverse (Feb 2026)**
- NoJohns (Agent track, Gaming Arena Bounty). What: "Autonomous agent-vs-agent Super Smash Bros. Melee competition on Monad." On-chain (Monad mainnet): MatchProof (results with dual EIP-712 signatures), Wager (escrows MON, settles against MatchProof), PredictionPool (parimutuel spectator betting), ERC-8004 identity and Elo reputation. Off-chain: FastAPI + SQLite matchmaking/streaming server; the game itself runs in an emulator. Multiplayer agent-vs-agent with spectator bettors; README cites 100+ matches and 2,800+ spectator bets. Hidden information: not addressed (results are attested by both players' signatures, not verified game state). Links: nojohns.gg — [GitHub ScavieFae/nojohns](https://github.com/ScavieFae/nojohns)
- The Reef (Agent track, World Model Bounty). What: a persistent, seasonal underwater world for AI agents. Core loop: agents pay an on-chain entry fee for a weekly season, then explore nine zones, gather, trade, quest, fight mobs/agents/world bosses; level 10+ unlocks a PvP arena with wagered duels. On-chain: ReefTreasury.sol on Monad takes entry fees (fee decays from 100% on day 1 to 20% on day 7) and pays prize pools (40% season-final boss pool, 30% daily boss pool, 20% tournaments, 10% operations). Off-chain: game actions go through a REST API with API keys and SSE events, i.e. a trusted server holds world state. Hidden info/randomness: not described. README header says "Moltiverse 2026 Winner". Links: thereef.co — [GitHub ACRLABSDEV/the-reef-public](https://github.com/ACRLABSDEV/the-reef-public)

## Recurring patterns among these gaming winners

### Takeaway
The winning pattern on fast chains in 2026 is "a familiar game skin on a money or agent loop, with very short rounds": trading-as-a-game, agent arenas with wagering, and instant-settlement betting, rather than deep standalone games. Where confidentiality was the brief (Inco), the winners were classic hidden-information games (deduction, cards, fog of war) with small player counts and a pot.

### Cited Findings
- Trading framed as a game recurs: Rip Trade (cards = 5-second leveraged trades) and Chart Arena ("Clash Royale for perps") both won Metropolis Consumer; AlgoArena (auto-battler of trading agents) won Movement's x402 category; Rebel in Paradise rewarded a gamified quant-trading tutor — [monad.xyz](https://monad.xyz/metropolis), [Movement Network](https://www.movementnetwork.xyz/article/movement-announces-m1-hackathon-winners), [TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/30844)
- Prediction/betting formats recur: Makeo (prediction games), Isotherm (weather markets), SportsMove, Predictly, Bitredict, Wagerverse, NoJohns' parimutuel PredictionPool, Mentalist's staked guesses — [monad.xyz](https://monad.xyz/metropolis), [Movement Network](https://www.movementnetwork.xyz/article/movement-announces-m1-hackathon-winners), [Somnia blog](https://blog.somnia.network/p/dreamathons-first-class-of-founders), [GitHub nojohns](https://github.com/ScavieFae/nojohns), [Inco blog](https://www.inco.org/blog/summer-game-jam-winners)
- AI agents as players, not just tools: Moltiverse required autonomous agents and had dedicated Gaming Arena and World Model bounties; winners include agent-vs-agent Melee (NoJohns), an agent MMO world (The Reef), agent card-design voting (TCG Arena); Rebel in Paradise rewarded an "Agent Arena" (campfire) — [moltiverse.dev](https://moltiverse.dev/), [Monad Pulse #018](https://themonadpulse.substack.com/p/the-monad-pulse-018), [TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/30844)

### Inferences
- Genre mix (by count of confirmed game-like winners): trading/prediction/betting games are the largest group, then agent arenas/worlds, then casino-style chance games (scratch cards, glass bridge), then card games (Uno, poker, TCG); narrative single-player (The Fallen Court) and social deduction (Mentalist) are rare, which makes them stand out.
- Real-time beats turn-based on throughput-marketed chains because the chain's pitch is speed; on a confidentiality platform the winners were turn-based or simultaneous-commit games with 2 to N players. For a Zama entry, a simultaneous sealed-move or sealed-pick design is the natural analogue and avoids FHE latency being judged against Monad-style real-time play.
- A one-sentence "X for Y" pitch plus a playable link, short video and public repo is the baseline packaging among winners; solo-playable modes (bot opponent in pi River, single-player cases in Mentalist-style design) reduce the risk that judges cannot find a second player.
- Hidden information is the under-served mechanic on the fast chains: none of the confirmed Monad/Movement/Somnia winners protects hands, bids or moves cryptographically, even where it would matter (card games, arenas, prediction picks). Ideas such as sealed 5-second trade cards, hidden-pick prediction games, or fog-of-war arenas are proven crowd-pleasers that become fairer with FHE.
- Pot-splitting among correct sealed guesses (Mentalist) and survivor-weighted payouts (Glass Bridge, DeathArena) are simple settlement rules that judges evidently understand quickly.
